PTA Reflections Art Program

Reflections Art Night · Friday, October 9 · 5:30 p.m.

Make art. Connect. Win prizes!

Join us in the Will Rogers Cafetorium for a hands-on evening of creating. Make a unique piece for your Reflections entry with the help of our volunteer experts, and get a shot at a $25 Blick Art Materials gift card just for coming!

  • No experience needed: every student, every idea, all six categories

Your creativity could go national!

Entries due Friday, October 16, 2026

Theme: “What My Culture Means to Me” · Dance, film, literature, music, photography & visual arts

Calling all Will Rogers artists!

Does your student love to draw, dance, sing, write, snap photos, or make movies? Reflections is their stage. Each year, over 300,000 students in Pre-K through Grade 12 create original works of art in response to a student-selected theme. This 50+ year-old National PTA program helps kids explore their own thoughts, feelings, and ideas, build confidence, and discover a love of creating that carries into everything they do.

Will Rogers students are invited to submit their completed works in one or all of the six arts categories:

  • Dance Choreography
  • Film Production
  • Literature
  • Music Composition
  • Photography
  • Visual Arts

Students participate in the division for their grade: Primary (Pre-K – Grade 2), Intermediate (Grades 3–5), Middle School (Grades 6–8), High School (Grades 9–12), and Special Artist (all grades welcome).*

*Students who identify as having a disability and may receive services under IDEA or ADA: Section 504 may enter in the Special Artist Division or the grade division most closely aligned to their functional abilities.

Ready to enter?

Read the guidelines for each category and the official rules, then bring your finished piece and entry form by Friday, October 16. Entry form and drop-off details are in the weekly newsletter. Questions? Email reflections@willrogerspta.com.
