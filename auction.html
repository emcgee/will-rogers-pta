Grown-Ups' Night Out & Live Auction Fundraiser

The WILL ROGERS GROWN-UPS' NIGHT OUT is coming up! Please join us!

🎉

Save the Dates

Online Auction: November 6–14, 2026

Grown-Ups’ Night Out celebration: Saturday, November 14 · 6:00 p.m.

Bid all week from anywhere, then join us to celebrate on the 14th!

Our auction runs November 6–14, with dining, travel, experiences, and one-of-a-kind treasures donated by local businesses and Will Rogers families. The week wraps up with our Grown-Ups’ Night Out celebration on Saturday, November 14. Tickets, venue, and bidding details will be announced soon — watch the weekly newsletter. Questions? Contact auction@willrogerspta.com.

Donations

We're still looking for donations of items, experiences, gift certificates, and more for our upcoming online and live auction. These donations are tax deductible and will support our school community in a big way. Every family's contribution counts, and together we can make this event a success! Please use the donation form (opens in a new tab), and reach out to fundraising@willrogerspta.com for any questions.
