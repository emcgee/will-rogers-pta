2026-27 PTA Meetings

☕

You're invited — in person

First Wednesday of the month

8:45–9:45 a.m. · Will Rogers Library

Drop off the kids and come on in! We put out fresh coffee and breakfast pastries for everyone. Can't make it in person? You can always join by Zoom.

PTA meetings are one of the friendliest, easiest ways to be part of Will Rogers — meet other families, say hi to our teachers and staff, and hear what's happening at our school. They run for just one hour, right after morning drop-off, and everyone is welcome whether or not you're a PTA member. There's nothing to prepare; just bring yourself.

📅

When

The first Wednesday of most months, 8:45–9:45 a.m., right after drop-off. The full list of dates is below.

📍

Where

In person in the Will Rogers school library, 2401 14th Street. Just come on in — we'll be so glad to see you.

🥐

Coffee & breakfast

We provide fresh coffee and breakfast pastries for everyone who comes. Show up hungry and stay for the conversation.

We'd truly love to see you in person — that's where the coffee, the conversations, and the sense of community happen. But mornings are busy, so a virtual option is always there when you need it.

Can't make it in person?

No problem — you can join any meeting by Zoom using the same link every month.

2026-27 meeting dates

Every meeting is 8:45–9:45 a.m. in the school library (with a Zoom option), except as noted on the calendar. Agendas are posted before each meeting.

  • September 2

    Our first meeting of the year! Meet the executive board, learn how PTA works at Will Rogers, and help adopt the 2026–2027 budget — a great first step to getting involved.

  • October 7

    Coffee, community, and a look at the fall's events and fundraisers.

  • November 4

    Hear how the year is going and what's coming up before the holidays.

  • January 6

    Kick off the new year with us and help shape our spring plans.

  • February 3

    Program updates, upcoming events, and time to connect with other families.

  • March 3

    Spring is busy — come hear what's ahead and how you can lend a hand.

  • April 7

    Updates on spring events and an early look toward next year.

  • May 5

    In & Out Meeting — our last meeting of the year. Officers transition and we adopt the 2027–28 budget. Come celebrate a wonderful year together!

Would you like the board to consider a specific item for an upcoming agenda? Just email the PTA president.

Meeting attendance and voting

Our meetings are open to all families and staff at Will Rogers Learning Community. Non-PTA members are always welcome to attend and listen via a courtesy seat provided by the president; however, per California State PTA, a guest who is not a member of the association may not make motions or take part in discussion, nominations, votes, or elections. Join PTA today and make your voice heard!
