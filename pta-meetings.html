Every meeting is 8:30–9:30 a.m. in the library, with Zoom, unless the calendar says otherwise. There’s no meeting in December. Agendas are posted before each meeting.

Want the board to consider something for an upcoming agenda? Email the PTA president.

Who can vote at meetings?Everyone can come; members can vote

Our meetings are open to all Will Rogers families and staff, and everyone is welcome to attend and listen. Under California State PTA rules (opens in a new tab), only members can make motions or take part in discussion, nominations, votes, or elections.
