    All year long

    Beyond the big events, the PTA runs and supports programs all year. Want to help with any of them? Lend a hand.

    • Fundraisers

      • Will Rogers Fund: our annual giving drive, and the most direct way to help. Donate
      • Read-A-Thon in the fall
      • Grown-Ups’ Night Out & Auction in November
      • Jog-a-Thon in the spring
      • Book Fair, twice a year, supporting our library
      • Student Store, most Fridays at the main entrance
      • Shop & Support: link your Ralphs account at ralphs.com/communityrewards (opens in a new tab) and search for Will Rogers PTA

    • Community & programs

      • Welcome Back Picnic, usually the first Friday of the school year
      • Movie Nights in the fall and spring
      • Family Dance in the spring
      • PTA Reflections arts program
      • Staff Appreciation, including Teacher Appreciation Week in May
      • Feeding Rogers Families, sorting and sharing food with families in need
      • Career Day, Bike It Walk It, Mildred’s Garden, Campus Beautification, and the Yearbook
      • 5th Grade Activities: a dance, a special outing, and a memory book

    • School events we support

      • STEM Expo family night, with star-gazing
      • Family Talent Show, usually the last Friday of the year
      • Cinco de Mayo, a 40+ year Will Rogers tradition
      • 5th Grade Culmination
      • Regenerative Farm volunteer support
      • School tours & kindergarten welcome

    The PTA also funds classroom volunteers, room representatives, enrichment, grants, scholarships, field trips, and much more.
