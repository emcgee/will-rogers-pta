Movie Night & Star Party is a wrap. Thanks for watching the stars with us!

Movie Night & Star Party

Fri, Oct 16, 2026

A family movie under the stars, with telescopes set up for stargazing. Bring a blanket, lawn chairs, and your crew!

See the full flyer (opens in a new tab)

Start time and other details will be in the Wednesday newsletter.

Oct16

What’s in store

  • A movie under the stars

    A family-friendly film on the big screen, outdoors, as the sky gets dark.

  • Telescopes & stargazing

    Line up for a look through the telescopes at the moon, the planets, and the stars.

  • Bring your crew

    Pack a blanket or lawn chairs and come with family and friends. Everyone is welcome.

Questions?

Email communications@willrogerspta.com, and watch the Wednesday newsletter for the latest.
