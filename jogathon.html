Jog-a-Thon 2027 is done. Thank you, runners, walkers, and donors!

Jog-a-Thon

Fri, March 19, 2027 · Track & field, during the school day

One of the biggest and most beloved days of the school year: every student and every class jogs, walks, or moves to raise money for our school.

Want to help plan it? Email the Jog-a-Thon team

Registration and volunteer sign-ups open closer to the event, and we’ll announce them in the Wednesday newsletter.

Mar19

How it works

Students build their own fundraising page to tell friends and family about their run. On the day, every class takes a turn on the track, and the whole school joins the assemblies and activities. The per-grade schedule and volunteer sign-ups will be shared closer to the event.

Prizes

2027 prizes will be announced closer to the event. Last year’s included:

  • For classes

    • Classes with 100% of students signed up get a popsicle party
    • The class that raises the most gets a pizza party

  • For students

    • Register a fundraising page and be entered in a weekly raffle
    • $20+: a squishy toy · $150+: a swag bag
    • $300+: an invitation to the Ice Cream Social
    • Top 5 fundraisers: a field trip with Principal Daruty

Thank you to last year’s sponsors
