Forms & flyers

The PTA forms families and volunteers ask for most, plus this season’s event flyers.

For families

  • Join the PTA

    Membership is $11 and there’s no paper form: you join online in about two minutes.

    Join for $11

  • Auction item donation form

    Donating something to the Grown-Ups’ Night Out auction? Fill this out and send it to the fundraising team.

    Download (PDF) (opens in a new tab)

For volunteers & committee chairs

  • Reimbursement & payment request

    Fill out the whole form before printing, then sign it and send it to the PTA treasurer with original receipts. All purchases must be budgeted and approved in advance.

    Download (PDF) (opens in a new tab)

  • SMMUSD facility use permit

    For PTA event chairs: apply through the district, and confirm details with the PTA president and treasurer before you submit.

    District permit page (opens in a new tab)

  • PTA officer nomination

    Elections are in March. A nominating committee forms in November or early January and presents a slate at least 28 days before the election.

    Download (PDF) (opens in a new tab)

  • Honorary Service Award nomination

    Each spring we recognize people whose service to students goes above and beyond. Nominations open in the spring, and awards are presented in May.
