September 14 – October 2, 2026

Read-A-Thon

Read More. Do More. A Brighter Tomorrow! Read-A-Thon 2026 runs September 14 through October 2 (extended by one week!). Students read at home, log their minutes, and gather pledges from family, friends, and neighbors. Every page read and every donation helps fund PTA programs, enrichment, and resources that make our school community stronger.

Register & Donate on RallyUp (opens in a new tab)

Registration is free and takes about a minute at willrogerspta.rallyup.com/462ac4 (opens in a new tab). Every registered reader earns a prize just for signing up! Questions? Contact enrichment@willrogerspta.com.

Prizes

  • Free registration prize — every student who registers gets one
  • Mini Reading Buddy
  • Top 3 fundraisers — book-themed lunch with Principal Daruty
  • Top reader — book bundle swag
  • Top 3 readers — Guest Reader role: read to a younger class
  • Top fundraising class — pizza party + storytime with Mr. Sea
  • Top minutes-read class — a book series for the classroom and the schoolwide Golden Book Traveling Trophy

We appreciate any donation! Donate online through your reader's RallyUp page, or by cash or check payable to Will Rogers PTA.

We're committed to providing our students with the tools they need to succeed, and we need your support. This year, we aim for 100% family participation.
