Read-A-Thon 2026 has wrapped up. Thank you, readers and donors!

Read-A-Thon

Sept 14 – Fri, Oct 2, 2026

Read more. Do more. A brighter tomorrow! Students read at home, log their minutes, and gather pledges from family and friends. Every page and every donation supports PTA programs.

Register & donate (opens in a new tab)

Registration is free, takes about a minute, and every registered reader earns a prize just for signing up.

Oct2

Prizes

  • Just for signing up

    • A free registration prize for every student who registers
    • A Mini Reading Buddy

  • Top readers & fundraisers

    • Top 3 fundraisers: a book-themed lunch with Principal Daruty
    • Top reader: a book bundle
    • Top 3 readers: become a Guest Reader for a younger class

  • Whole classes

    • Top fundraising class: a pizza party plus storytime with Mr. Sea
    • Top minutes-read class: a book series for the classroom and the schoolwide Golden Book Traveling Trophy

Every gift counts

Donate online through your reader’s RallyUp page, or by cash or check payable to Will Rogers PTA. Donors encourage students, students read, and the PTA raises funds for programs, enrichment, and resources that make our school stronger. This year, we’re aiming for 100% family participation.
