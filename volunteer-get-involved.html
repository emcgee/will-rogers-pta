Lend a hand

Every helping hand makes Will Rogers brighter, and you choose how much or how little you do. One event, one committee, or one morning a month: it all counts. Here are three easy ways to start.

Three ways to start

  • Join the volunteer list

    Fill out our short form and we’ll share flexible, one-and-done ways to pitch in. Never any pressure.

    Fill out the form (opens in a new tab)

  • Email our volunteer team

    Tell us what you enjoy or how much time you have, and we’ll help you find a good fit.

    volunteers@willrogerspta.com

  • Come to a PTA meeting

    The first Wednesday of the month, 8:30 a.m. in the library, with coffee. The easiest way to meet everyone.

    Meeting dates

Where we could use you right now

These roles are open this year. Curious? Ask, with no commitment.

  • Membership Chair

    Help families join the PTA and support our room representatives.

    Ask about it

  • 6th Vice President of Community Concerns

    Lead advocacy, health & safety, Safe Routes to School, the Green Team, and more.

    Ask about it

  • Cinco de Mayo Festival chair

    Work with the school to plan our 40+ year spring celebration.

    Ask about it

  • Family Dance chair

    Plan the fall and spring family dances: music, snacks, decorations, and fun.

    Ask about it
