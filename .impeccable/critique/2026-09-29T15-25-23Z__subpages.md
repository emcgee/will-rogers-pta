## Design Specificity Verdict
Two pages are authored for Will Rogers: membership (the $11 hero, "Joining is not a to-do list") and pta-meetings (coffee and pastries after drop-off, warm Spanish). Read-A-Thon's first phone screen also works. Everything else is Wix-era template in the site's colors: a flat teal title bar, prose, emoji-tile card grids, and thick-edged callouts. Next to the new homepage they look like an older site. The homepage invites; the interior pages post notices. The homepage-to-interior handoff breaks promises:
- "Ways to volunteer" names no ways to volunteer.
- "Every sign-up from the Wednesday newsletter" (/links) holds zero sign-ups.
- "Latest news" has no news.
- Reflections offers no way to enter.
- The auction page has nothing to do.

## Priority Issues
[P0] The 404 page breaks exactly where old links land. All asset paths are relative, so any bad URL one folder deep (/es/anything, old Wix /post/... and /event-details/...) renders unstyled with broken nav. es/404.html is never served, so Spanish visitors always get English. Fix: root-absolute paths in 404.html, a bilingual message with shortcuts (Events, Meetings, Join, Donate). Command: harden.

[P1] Giving and joining are buried and contradictory.
- The donate button sits about 1,822px down on a phone, below an Ed Foundation policy argument.
- The fund goal is $30,000 "Will Rogers Fund" on donate but $20,000 "Annual Fund" on joinandgive.
- There are five labels for one Totem link and two competing join pages (joinandgive, orphaned membership-form).
- Forms promises a downloadable membership form that doesn't exist, with cash/check instructions.
Fix: ask first (gold "Donate to the PTA" plus a 501(c)(3) line in the first screen), fold the Ed Foundation explainer, one fund name and goal (needs PTA confirmation), retire joinandgive/membership-form into /membership, fix or remove the forms entry. Command: distill, then clarify.

[P1] Homepage promises not kept. Volunteer page = one form button plus 18 footer links. /links = no dated sign-ups; social cards first; first PTA row at about 1,165px. News = no news. Reflections = no entry form or drop-off. Auction = no button (item donation is an inline PDF link). Fix: /links gets a dated "This week" block driven by the homepage's data-start/data-end script; volunteer names 3 real first steps (content from the PTA); Reflections and auction get a single gold action. Command: onboard, then layout.

[P1] Interior pages don't know the date.
- Meetings shows the past Sept 2 meeting as current; the Zoom button is about 2,500px down; the next meeting (Oct 7) isn't surfaced.
- The events page's "This Wednesday" will go stale on Oct 1.
- Jog-a-thon shows last year's sponsors and incentives with no year.
- Reflections rules cite 2021.
- The calendar is a Google Doc iframe unreadable on a phone (about 5px text), English-only, with nothing for screen readers.
Fix: extend site.js's date logic to meetings ("Next meeting" card) and the events page; mark past items; an HTML calendar list or at least a proper summary. Command: harden.

[P2] Legacy system everywhere.
- Title-bar heroes and emoji tile grids; 65 accent-bordered cards.
- Eyebrows and several gold pills per band.
- The events hub is 25 undated cards over 12.5k px; contact is 30 equal cards over 10.5k px.
- Heading skips on 10 pages.
- Pre-existing EN/ES drift: the Spanish pages have gold heading bars that English lacks.
Fix: bring the flyer-wall language to events-fundraisers (dated posters), a "Who do I ask?" router on contact, one gold per band, and consistent headings across EN and ES. Command: layout, then quieter.
