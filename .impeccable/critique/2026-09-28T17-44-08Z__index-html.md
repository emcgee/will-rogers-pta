## Design Specificity Verdict

## Overall Impression

The paint is right and the structure is generic. It has a coherent, joyful palette that avoids the institutional and clip-art failure modes. But the page is built as a nonprofit brochure, when its real job is to be the Wednesday-newsletter landing page. The biggest opportunity is to put "what's happening this week" first. The most urgent defect is that the mobile menu is broken in production.

## What's Working

- **Event cards.** Each card goes date pill → title → full-width button → secondary link, which answers "when, and where do I tap" inside one card. The ink "hot" pill is a cheap, clear urgency signal.
- **A coherent, logo-true system.** Cream base, tinted shadows, logo-only colors, and Fredoka/Poppins/Nunito each with a job. The four-color stripe and cream wave are tasteful signatures.
- **Real bilingual parity.** /es/ mirrors every block, including alt text and aria-labels, in warm tú Spanish. The longer strings fit at 390px.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The mobile and tablet menu is broken.**
- **What:** at ≤1080px, opening ☰ squeezes the nav into a clipped column beside the logo. It is not a full-width panel. Labels are cut off ("PTA Info & Mee…"), the header grows to about 765px tall, and Donate and Español sit at the bottom of a clipped, internally scrolling strip.
- **Cause:** `.site-nav` has `flex-basis: 100%`, but `.header-inner` never sets `flex-wrap: wrap`. This is confirmed in `css/style.css`.
- **Also:** the Spanish desktop logo is squished horizontally by the longer nav.
- **Why it matters:** the primary user is on a phone. On mobile, the menu is the only route to Español, Volunteer, Forms and Meetings. Laptop users zoomed to 200% hit the same bug.
- **Fix:**
  - Add `flex-wrap: wrap` to `.header-inner` inside the ≤1080px query.
  - Add `flex-shrink: 0` to `.site-logo`.
  - Toggle `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` in site.js.
  - Make the ☰ hit area 44×44 (it is currently 44×35).
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden (then /impeccable adapt).

**[P1] The first phone screen brands and asks instead of answering.**
- **What:** at 390×844 the hero fills the screen:
  - an eyebrow;
  - an h1 that repeats the logo;
  - a 3-line subhead;
  - full-width Join and Donate buttons.

  This week's event only peeks in at the bottom, and the four quick tiles carry equal weight, so "this Wednesday" looks like "November".
- **Why it matters:** it breaks "answer the parent's question in one tap" for newsletter arrivals, and it asks for money before giving anything.
- **Fix:**
  - Shorten the mobile hero to about 50vh.
  - Make the h1 work, e.g. "This week at Will Rogers", or pull the quick strip into the hero.
  - Give only the this-week tile the ink "hot" treatment and let the others recede.
  - Keep Join/Donate as secondary.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout (with /impeccable distill).

**[P1] "The Sun Is the Button" has collapsed, and date pills are unreadable.**
- **What:** there are about 14 gold pills on the page plus 4 saturated quick tiles, so primary and tertiary actions look the same. Separately, the specificity bug above turns every date pill gray.
- **Why it matters:** a distracted parent can't find the single main action. The date, the most important fact on each card, fails contrast.
- **Fix:**
  - Allow one gold pill per band.
  - Make the card-grid buttons arrow text links, as the event cards already do.
  - Make the quick tiles white with a colored edge, except the this-week tile.
  - Fix the date-pill color with `.event-card__body .event-card__date`.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable quieter (then /impeccable polish).

**[P2] Contrast failures across the page.**
- **What:**
  - White on cyan at 2.8:1: the Read-A-Thon quick tile, "Register & Donate", and the CTA band's cyan end.
  - Cyan-600 eyebrows, the active nav item, "More →" links and the award "Phoebe Apperson Hearst" line at 3.2–3.9:1.
  - Nav hover at 3.5:1.
  - `.btn-sage:hover` at 4.4:1.
- **Also:** there are no `:focus-visible` styles at all, so keyboard focus relies on thin browser defaults against gold pills.
- **Why it matters:** these are AA failures on the main page, for parents reading outdoors on phones.
- **Fix:**
  - Ink text on the cyan fill, or a teal-700 fill.
  - Keep the CTA-band gradient from teal to teal-700.
  - Use teal-700 for small cyan text.
  - Add a house focus ring: 3px ink outline, 3px offset.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable audit (then /impeccable colorize).

**[P2] The bottom half is redundant and long.**
- **What:**
  - Welcome, Stay-in-the-loop, the CTA band and the 6-card grid all repeat the nav and hero calls to action.
  - The page runs about 11,600px on mobile.
  - The reassurance copy ($11, tax-deductible, what donations fund) is buried in the 9th section.
  - The card copy is boilerplate.
- **Why it matters:** scroll fatigue, and the persuasion comes too late.
- **Fix:**
  - Merge Welcome, the CTA band and the card grid into one "Find your place" block: Join ($11, you get a vote) · Volunteer · Give (tax-deductible).
  - Make Meetings and Contact a compact row.
  - Cut the copy to one sentence each.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill (then /impeccable clarify).

## Minor Observations

- The School of Excellence seal appears in both the header and the award band. At 50–62px its text is illegible.
- The logo JPG shows as a white box on the cream header. A transparent PNG or SVG would fix it.
- Desktop dropdown items render center-aligned.
- The award paragraph is centered across about 9 lines on mobile, and runs about 97 characters per line on desktop.
- 4:3 top-anchored crops cut through flyer text (the Reflections "What My CULTURE" flyer). Use `--fit` for text-heavy flyers.
- `.card` lifts on hover although only its button is clickable, which violates the Tap-Lift Rule.
- Naming drift: "Will Rogers Elementary PTA" in the footer.
- "501(c)3" should be "501(c)(3)".
- The Twitter bird should be X.
- One straight apostrophe in "There's", where the rest of the page uses curly quotes.
- The IB strip is a full band for one sentence.
- The mobile hero eyebrow is 0.72rem tracked caps, which is too small.
