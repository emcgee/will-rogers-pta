That's it — for the whole year

Membership is just $11 per person, and anyone who loves a Will Rogers kid can join — from grandparents to every one of your children.

Join in 2 Minutes

Membership is for your whole family

Grandparents, parents, caregivers, aunts, uncles, big brothers and little sisters — if they love a Will Rogers student, they can be a member. Teachers, staff, and neighbors, too! Each membership is just $11, and you can sign up as many family members as you like.

👵 Grandparents 👨‍👩‍👧 Parents & caregivers 🧑 Aunts, uncles & friends 🧒 Every one of your kids 🍎 Teachers & staff 🏡 Neighbors

Joining is not a to-do list

We hear this one a lot: “If I join, won't the PTA expect me to volunteer, show up, or do more?” — Nope! Membership is simply your way of adding your voice and your support. What's expected of you after you join? Honestly… nothing.

😌

It's all optional

  • Volunteering or helping out
  • Attending meetings
  • Fundraising or selling
  • Any extra donations
  • Any time commitment
🎉

You simply get to…

  • Vote on decisions for our school
  • Add your voice to what we do
  • Enjoy State & National PTA member perks
  • Help every kid at Will Rogers
  • Be part of the community

Want to do more? Wonderful — there's a place for you. But it's 100% optional, always.

What your $11 makes possible

Your membership fuels the programs, events, and support that make Will Rogers special — and it strengthens our collective voice for kids. PTA is the largest children's advocacy organization in the nation, and every member makes it stronger.

🎪

Community & fun

Movie nights, family dances, the Cinco de Mayo celebration, the Welcome Back Picnic, and so much more.

📚

Classroom support

Supplemental supplies, library support, field trips, and teacher grants for every classroom.

📣

A voice for kids

Advocacy for students at the local, state, and national level — and a bigger membership makes it louder.

A note from our PTA President

August 2026

Dear Will Rogers Learning Community Families,

Welcome to the 2026–2027 school year at Will Rogers! I’m Evan, your PTA President, and I’m so excited to kick off another year of learning, laughter, and community. Whether you’re brand new to our school or a familiar face, we’re thrilled to have you as part of the Will Rogers family.

The PTA is at the heart of so much that makes our school special. We host movie nights under the stars, throw family dances, celebrate Cinco de Mayo, support our amazing regenerative farm, and bring countless other events and programs to life. These moments aren’t just fun — they create connections, memories, and opportunities that shape our children’s elementary school experience.

Here’s the thing: the PTA is powered entirely by parents, caregivers, and community members like you. And joining is the easiest way to help — no strings attached. Whether you ever lift a finger beyond that is completely up to you; every membership counts all on its own.

Your membership fuels the programs, resources, and events that keep our school vibrant. We’d also love to see you at our PTA meetings — they’re an amazing chance to stay in the loop, share your voice, and meet other incredible families — but there’s never any pressure.

Let’s make 2026–2027 a year to remember. Together, we can create an even stronger, more connected Will Rogers community — and have a ton of fun along the way.

Here’s to an amazing year,
Evan McGee
President, Will Rogers Learning Community PTA
